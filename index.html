FAQ

Questions we hear often

Does a root canal hurt?

With modern numbing and gentle technique, a root canal feels similar to having a filling done. It is done to remove pain, not cause it.

Can children be treated at your clinic?

Yes. We offer pediatric dentistry with a gentle, step-by-step approach, from first check-ups to cavity care and preventive treatment.

How do I know if I need a root canal?

Lingering pain, night-time toothache, swelling, a darkened tooth or strong sensitivity to heat can be signs. Only an exam and X-ray can confirm, so please visit us.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call us right away on 96777 55087 or 63838 92493. For a knocked-out tooth, hold it by the crown, do not scrub it, and bring it with you.
