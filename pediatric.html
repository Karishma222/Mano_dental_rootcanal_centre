Daily habits

Tips for parents

🪥

Brush twice a day

Use a pea-sized amount of fluoride toothpaste and help children under 8.

🍼

No bedtime bottle

Milk or juice left on teeth overnight causes early cavities.

🍎

Smart snacks

Choose fruit, curd and milk. Keep sweets to meal times.

📅

Visit every 6 months

Small problems are easy to fix when found early.

FAQ

Parents ask

When should my child first visit?

By the first birthday or when the first tooth appears.

My child is scared. What should I do?

Stay calm and positive and avoid words like pain or injection. We take time to build trust before any treatment.

Do milk teeth need treatment?

Yes. They hold space for permanent teeth and help your child eat, speak and smile.
